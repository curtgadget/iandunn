# Impeccable — Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
iandunn.net is the personal portfolio of **Ian Dunn** — engineering manager at
Pinterest, former founder, indie builder. Visitors are recruiters and hiring
managers, potential collaborators, engineering peers, founders building
sideline/sports tooling, and people who have just met Ian and want to know who
he is. They arrive on desktop and phone, usually briefly, to answer one
question: *who is this person, and what has he made?*

### Use cases
- Get a fast, credible read on Ian's career and character.
- Browse his apps — FutAve, TheraBasics, Center Ref.
- Reach him (email, book a call, LinkedIn).
- Read his writing (a plain dated list; one post so far).

### Aesthetic Direction
**The dealt system** (November 2026 redesign, recorded in
`docs/pare/full-redesign/deal.md`). The look was dealt by the
`design-taste-frontend` taste-skill from randomized lists — dealing, not
hand-picked taste, is the mechanism for not looking AI-made. Build inside it;
never re-deal, never hand-steer it back toward defaults.

- Palette `oxblood-grey`: neutral stone ground `#e2e2e2`, surface `#d6d6d6`,
  one deep oxblood `#7d0c14` for text, links and the action. The footer is the
  one inverse (oxblood) ground. The colour on the page comes from the kept
  portraits and real app screenshots.
- Type `nunito`: Nunito alone, display 400 with tight tracking, text 400/500.
  No second face, no mono label voice, no display serif.
- Parts: split heroes, nav-centre, footer-dark + close-photo, surface-pairs
  (the band on Home only), arrow buttons, the initial mark, radius soft, one
  dealt scroll/entrance moment per page.

### Hard rules
1. **Never violet; no olive/sage** (TheraBasics separation); never back to
   cream + terracotta, mono eyebrow labels, or the accent-period flourish.
2. **The kit in `public/assets` is never edited** — only `src/styles/global.css`
   holds the project's `:root` tokens, exactly as dealt.
3. **Honest material only** — no invented numbers, testimonials or fake
   screenshots; real contact handles stay.
4. **Plain speech** — facts in the first person, no slogans or virtue words;
   every visitor-facing word lives in `docs/pare/full-redesign/copy.md` first.
5. **Photographs repeat only across pages, never within one.**
